FAQ

Common questions

What is post-quantum cryptography?

Post-quantum cryptography refers to algorithms designed to resist attacks from both classical and quantum computers. It is the practical path to protecting data today.

Why start migration now?

Encrypted data captured today can be decrypted once quantum computers mature. Migrating early reduces long-term exposure and avoids costly last-minute changes.

Do you work with existing cloud providers?

Yes. We design quantum-safe controls across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and hybrid environments without disrupting current operations.

How long does an assessment take?

Most initial cryptographic risk assessments take two to four weeks depending on the size and complexity of your environment.
